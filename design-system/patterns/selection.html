Selection

Selection answers “what did I pick?”. Focus answers “where am I typing?”. They are different questions and never share a treatment. Selection is a neutral fill in two tiers, and the tier with focus adds a 2px neutral edge in the subtle border. No accent at all: the accent is reserved for the primary action, so a chosen row never outranks the one thing worth clicking. (The edge was the accent until 2026-11-04, when a green outline round the row being read proved the loudest mark on every list.)

The edge is also the channel status may not borrow: a tinted row says what happened here, the edge says where you are. A row can wear both at once, which is the point — before the split, a green “finished while you were away” ring outshouted the neutral fill on the row the person was actually driving.

Three panes shouting at once, each with an accent fill and a left bar. Nothing says which list has focus, three signals carry one state, and the accent no longer means “this is the action”. Selection takes a neutral edge on one pane only, never an accent fill, and never a left bar.

Where a left bar is still correct

Only on a collapsed icon rail, where rows are too narrow for a fill to read. Everywhere else — every content list — selection is a fill.
